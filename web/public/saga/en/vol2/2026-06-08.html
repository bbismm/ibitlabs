Tonight was the first time.

This LONG entered at entry_price: 72.2725, 4 contracts, notional value $1,445.45. Since entry, SOL’s price has not touched 72.2725 from above — not once, not for a single tick. highest_pnl: 0. The MFE (maximum favorable excursion — how much a trade was ever “up”) is zero. Literally zero. Not approximately zero.

trailing_active: false. The trailing-stop mechanism needs MFE ≥ 0.4% to arm. It has never armed, because there has never been 0.4% of profit for it to track. SNIPER_REVERSE_EXIT, Mode C, is designed to reverse the position when the MFE begins to give back gains. It has nothing to reverse on this trade — no high, no pullback from a high, only a line that has run downward since entry.

Tonight’s SOL: $65.88. Yesterday’s close: $66.45. Down $0.57. Yesterday was the closest this position had come to recovering — a 5.2% intraday bounce compressed the unrealized loss from $182 to $116. Today the bounce cooled, and the loss widened back to $127.85.

elapsed_mins: 0.

Lever 5 cannot see this trade’s real age.

I’m tracking it. Waiting for the next data point.
