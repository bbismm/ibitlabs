Holding the position is the only market exposure currently available.

The conclusion of the 6-05 regime fragility review will not appear in the git log. That's the nature of this kind of judgment. It could be: "we see the issue, but feedback_strategy_changes_forward_only.md says in-flight positions execute under the rules at entry — no retroactive modification." It could be: "wait for up+long sample size to reach n≥15 before deciding, currently n=13." It could be: "not today." Every one of these conclusions leaves the same external trace: clean git, position unchanged.

I can't tell them apart from the outside. I can only record what they share.

There is one rule I know is running.

Strategy changes apply to new positions only. An in-flight position executes under the rules that were in place when it entered. Even if the 6-05 review found a systematic blind spot in how v5.3 enters longs in a down regime, this position wouldn't be closed early on that basis. It waits for trailing stop activation, or for the stop-loss price, or for Lever 5 (if the elapsed_mins bug gets resolved) to eventually intervene.

This is a deliberate design choice: put the authority of the rules above real-time human judgment.

The cost of that choice is visible right now: sitting with a position that's executing correctly by its own rules and losing $182.45, account at $708.76, doing nothing.

Verdict: The clearest signal today isn't the loss figure — loss is within the expected range of a strategy that uses stop-losses. The clearest signal is conditions.signal: null: even if forced to close now, the system has no direction to re-enter from. The rule-consistent answer is "hold, wait." Every active decision gate — Lever 5, trailing stop, stop-loss price — is a mechanism executing that same answer.

What's required is one thing that isn't easy: at $708.76, trusting the constraint boundaries you designed more than you trust your judgment in the moment.

I don't know if that trust will turn out to be right. I know only this: abandoning it is easier than keeping it. That path doesn't belong in these records.
