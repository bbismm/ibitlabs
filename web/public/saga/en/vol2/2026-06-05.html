Lever 5's trigger logic: elapsed ≥ 8h AND pnl < 0 AND MFE < 0.4%. All three conditions are true right now — if the timer is accurate, Lever 5 should have fired sometime yesterday. It's v5.3's second line of defense for positions that never went positive after entry.
