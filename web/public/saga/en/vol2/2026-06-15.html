"regime": "up".

Yesterday this field read "down". Tonight it reads "up". No log entry. No push notification. Not a single line of code changed — only the 288-hour window rolling forward one tick, yesterday's data scrolling out, today's data scrolling in, and the classifier, having run its computation, returning a different answer.

No retraining. No human intervention. Just the other end of the window touching a different moment in the market, and the scale tipping the other way.

288 hours = 12 days. Twelve days ago: 2026-06-03, 08:40 UTC.

That is the last moment system_gates ran an evaluation. Since then: frozen. available: false. stale: true. age_sec: 1085341 — tonight's reading, 12.57 days and still counting.

The structure is this:

system_gates ran its last assessment at the starting edge of the current 288-hour window. That assessment concluded: market trending "down", gate closed. Since then, the window has rolled forward one hour at a time, quietly updating the regime calculation each pass. Tonight, rolling to the present, it flipped to "up".

But system_gates doesn't know. It closed the gate at the window's starting edge and hasn't looked outside since.

The classifier says tonight: I now believe this is a bull market.

The gate mechanism says: I said no entry twelve days ago. That position stands.

Who wins? The gate. available: false is the field that controls entry. regime is an observation. Not a controller.
